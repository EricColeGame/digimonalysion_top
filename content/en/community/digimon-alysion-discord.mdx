## Digimon Alysion Discord Etiquette: How to Be a Good Community Member

Every large game server lives or dies on tone. The Digimon Alysion Discord will grow fast, and growth always brings the same friction: repeated questions, rumor amplification, and the occasional bad-faith actor. You can be part of the solution with a handful of habits.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Search before asking | Repost the same question in three channels |
| Cite where a rumor came from | Present speculation as confirmed news |
| Keep criticism specific and useful | Turn a balance complaint into a pile-on |
| Report scams to moderators | Confront suspected scammers publicly |
| Use spoiler tags for story content | Drop unrevealed card art without warning |

Safety deserves its own paragraph. No legitimate staff member will ever DM you asking for your password, your login code, or payment details. Turn on two-factor authentication, keep your account email private, and treat any "free pre-registration code" offer as suspicious until an official channel confirms it. Scam accounts often copy an official avatar and slightly misspell the handle, so check the username character by character.

Finally, remember that moderators are usually volunteers or community managers doing a job in their spare time. A polite DM about a problem tends to get a much better response than a public callout.

## Making the Server Work for You Long Term

Once launch hype settles, the value of any community shifts from news to people.
