## What Is Digimon Alysion, Exactly?

Digimon Alysion is an online card game published under the Bandai umbrella and built for mobile devices. It is free-to-play with in-app purchases, and pre-registration is already live through the App Store and Google Play. If you have been waiting for a Digimon card game that lives on your phone rather than in a deck box on your shelf, this is that project.

The framing is unusually story-driven for a card game. You follow Kanata Hondo, a veteran gamer invited by an online friend named Futre to try a hot new release called *Digimon Liberator*. In that fiction, players battle with a deck case-shaped gadget called a D-STORAGE while wearing a household full-dive VR headset known as a T.A.L.E., meeting friends and rivals inside a metaverse space called LACUNA. A second listed character, Valner Dragnogh, rounds out the revealed cast.

## Digimon Alysion Beta Gameplay: What the Closed Test Actually Covered

## What the Beta Feedback Means for Launch

The pre-registration campaign is the clearest signal yet that Bandai intends to convert test feedback into a launch-ready build rather than restart from scratch. For a free-to-play mobile card game, that usually means the combat and interface are considered broadly settled, and the remaining work sits in progression pacing, monetization balance, and content volume.

For the community, the practical advice is to temper two instincts. The first is assuming launch is imminent just because pre-registration is open — campaigns can run for a long stretch. The second is treating the closed test as a demo; it was a tuning exercise, and the version you eventually download will not be the version testers saw.

What you can reasonably expect, based on the official description, is a mobile card game with a story campaign set in LACUNA, a deck-building loop built around the D-STORAGE fiction, and a free-to-play economy with in-app purchases. Whether the Digimon Alysion beta gameplay feedback translated into a better economy is the question only launch will answer.
