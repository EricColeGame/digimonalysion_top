If your phone fails more than two of these checks, start thinking about an upgrade — not because Digimon Alysion is demanding, but because it's a long-term service you'll want to play comfortably.

## Reading Between the Lines: What "Mobile" Really Means

Community reports and player experience from similar Bandai Namco mobile releases suggest a familiar pattern: the game will likely install on a broad range of devices but perform best on hardware from the last three to four years. That's an expectation, not a published requirement.
