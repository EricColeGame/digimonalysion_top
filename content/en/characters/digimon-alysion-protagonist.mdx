## Who Is the Digimon Alysion Protagonist?

The Digimon Alysion protagonist is the lens that makes the rest of the cast click into place — your entry point into a story about guild politics, a secretive online friend, and a snow-white Digimon with a gem set into its forehead. If you are deciding whether this mobile card game's story is worth your attention, or you simply want to know who you will be spending hours with, everything starts with one cheerful beginner. Below is a breakdown of who the Digimon Alysion protagonist appears to be, how the supporting cast orbits around them, and what the official material still leaves open.

The strongest evidence comes from the [official Digimon Alysion character page](https://www.digimon-alysion.com/en/character/), which introduces four figures: Kanata Hondo, Futre, Valner Dragnogh, and Gemmon. Only one of them is written the way a player stand-in usually is.

### The Case for Kanata Hondo

Kanata Hondo is described as a casual gamer who is cheerful and energetic. She has dabbled in plenty of genres, but she is a complete beginner when it comes to card games. That specific combination — broad gaming literacy paired with zero card-game experience — is textbook protagonist design. It gives the writers a natural excuse to explain rules, terminology, and match flow to the player without ever breaking the fiction.

There is also a structural clue. Futre's profile refers to "our protagonist" as the person Futre is close to online. Kanata is the only character written in that player-facing register, which strongly implies she is the one you inhabit.

### The Honest Caveat

Bandai has not stamped a formal "main character" label on anyone. The site presents a cast, not a hierarchy, so the reasonable position is this: the Digimon Alysion protagonist is almost certainly Kanata Hondo based on how the official copy is framed, but treat it as a well-supported reading rather than a hard confirmation until the game launches.

One more detail worth flagging: the Japanese-language page carries the name 本堂アスカ (Hondo Asuka) in its image alt text, while the English page renders the character as Kanata Hondo. Localization quirks like this are common in pre-release material, so do not be surprised if you see both names floating around fan spaces.

## FAQ

**Is Kanata Hondo the Digimon Alysion protagonist?**

Based on the official character page, yes — she is the only figure described in player-facing terms, and Futre is explicitly framed as an online friend of "our protagonist." Bandai has not used the word "protagonist" as a formal label, so treat this as the reading the source material supports.

**Who is Futre in Digimon Alysion?**

Futre is a girl who is an online friend of the Digimon Alysion protagonist. She is close to the main character but appears to be hiding a secret. She is also a former member of the Di:WO guild, which its master Valner Dragnogh wants her to rejoin.

**Is Gemmon the protagonist's partner Digimon?**

That has not been confirmed. The official page only describes Gemmon as a mysterious snow-white Digimon with a unique gem on its forehead. Community reports frequently assume a partnership with the lead, but no official source states it.

**What kind of game is Digimon Alysion?**

It is an online card game for mobile, offered as free-to-play with in-app purchases. Because the title is still in development, the official site notes that images shown may differ from the finished product — so character details and features could still change before release.
