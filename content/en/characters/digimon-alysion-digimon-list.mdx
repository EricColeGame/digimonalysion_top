| Stage | Typical job in a deck | Resource profile | What to prioritize |
|---|---|---|---|

## Building Your First Team From the Digimon Alysion Digimon List

You do not need the finished roster to start planning. You need a template. The build below is a generic, role-based skeleton that you can slot confirmed cards into the moment the Digimon Alysion Digimon list grows.

1. **Pick your partner first.** Choose the partner Digimon whose evolution line you actually enjoy, because that card defines your deck's identity.
2. **Lock four copies of the partner.** Consistency beats variety in a 50-ish card style deck.
3. **Fill the low end.** Add 10–12 cheap Rookie-level cards, with at least half of them searching or drawing.
4. **Build the middle.** Add 8–10 Champion-level cards that pressure or trade efficiently.
5. **Choose a top end.** Add 4–6 Ultimate and Mega cards, no more.
6. **Add interaction.** Reserve 6–8 slots for removal or disruption.
7. **Test, then cut.** Remove the card you are happiest to see in your opening hand last — that is usually the one you actually need.
