## Digimon Alysion Gacha vs Other Monetization Models

Not every digital card game monetizes the same way, and knowing the alternatives helps you judge whether a given offer is fair.

| Model | How It Works | Player Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Random card gacha | Currency buys randomized pulls from a pool | High variance; whales and lucky players pull ahead |
| Crafting and dusting | Duplicates break down into currency for specific cards | Softens bad luck; rewards collection planning |
| Battle pass | Fixed price for a seasonal reward track | Predictable value for regular players |
| Direct card purchase | Buy a specific card outright | Removes luck entirely; often premium-priced |
| Starter and structure decks | One-time purchase of a prebuilt deck | Best entry point for new players |

Most successful mobile card games combine two or three of these. A hybrid — gacha plus crafting plus a battle pass — is the most likely shape for Digimon Alysion, though the final economy will only be clear after launch.

## A Practical Pulling Plan That Saves Money

You do not need a spreadsheet to pull responsibly. You need rules set before the banner appears.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finish the tutorial and story content before pulling | Maximizes free currency before you spend anything |
| 2 | Pick one deck archetype and commit | Concentrates your pulls on cards you will actually use |
| 3 | Set a monthly budget in real currency | Prevents impulse spending during banner hype |
| 4 | Skip the first limited banner unless it fits your deck | Launch banners are rarely the best long-term value |
| 5 | Save for banners with guaranteed or pity rewards | Reduces variance and protects your currency |
| 6 | Track your pull history | Reveals your real luck instead of your remembered luck |

### Bullet-point rules worth taping to your monitor

- Never pull while tilted after a loss streak.
- Treat premium currency as savings, not as a checking account.
- Read the rates before you spend; if the pool is not disclosed, wait.
- Spend on decks, not on individual cards you saw in a highlight reel.
- If a banner does not list a pity system, assume there is none.
