### Step 1–2: Setup and the Scripted Battle

Claim pre-registration rewards immediately — they usually expire into your inventory rather than staying claimable forever. Then play the scripted battle slowly. Do not skip animations; the first match is teaching you where the evolution button lives, how the deck case interface works, and how the turn timer behaves. Speed-running this is the single most common mistake in any Digimon Alysion card game tutorial.

### Step 3–4: Read Cards, Then Play

Reading your own starter deck end to end is unglamorous and effective. You want to answer three questions: how does this deck get ahead, what does it do when it falls behind, and which card is the win condition? If you cannot answer all three, you are not ready for ranked.

### Step 5–6: Simplify, Then Test

Cut your deck to one plan. Beginner decks lose because they try to do three things. Then take it into real matches, where opponents will punish every inefficiency your AI practice partner ignored.

## Deck-Building Tips for New Players

You do not need a meta list to start. You need consistency. These principles apply to almost any online card game and will hold up even as Digimon Alysion's card pool expands.

- **Pick one evolution line and commit.** Splashy off-theme cards feel great and draw dead.
- **Count your cheap cards.** Early plays matter more than finishers in beginner matches.
- **Cap your situational cards.** If a card only works when you are already winning, cut it.
- **Test in blocks of five matches.** Change one thing, play five games, evaluate honestly.
- **Track your losses.** Write down the turn you lost control; patterns appear fast.

| Deck Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too many themes | Inconsistent openings | Cut to one evolution line |
| Too expensive | Slow starts | Add lower-cost cards |
| Too reactive | No pressure | Add proactive threats |
| Too many one-ofs | Unreliable draws | Raise counts on key cards |
| No win condition | Long games, no finish | Identify and double down on a closer |

## FAQ

**Is Digimon Alysion free to play?**
Yes. The official site lists it as free-to-play with in-app purchases, on mobile platforms. You can learn the game and play matches without spending, which is exactly what a Digimon Alysion card game tutorial should encourage at first.

**Do I need the T.A.L.E. headset or D-STORAGE gadget to play?**
Those are story elements in the game's fiction, not hardware you buy. The game itself runs on mobile. Think of them the way you think of a duel disk in a card anime.

**How long should the tutorial take?**
Budget around 90 minutes for setup, the scripted battle, reading your starter deck, and three practice matches. Rushing this stage is the main reason new players stall in ranked.

**Is Digimon Alysion the same as the physical Digimon Card Game?**
They are related but distinct. Alysion is a separate online card game whose in-universe title is Digimon Liberator. Use the in-game tutorial as your source of truth rather than assuming tabletop rules transfer directly.

**Where should I follow updates?**
The official site's news section, plus the linked official social channels. Balance changes and campaign details appear there first, and a good Digimon Alysion card game tutorial should always point you back to primary sources.
