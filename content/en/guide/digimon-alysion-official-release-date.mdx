## What Is Digimon Alysion?

Digimon Alysion is a free-to-play online card game built for mobile devices. Rather than a straight port of the physical Digimon Card Game, it wraps the card battles inside a story-driven world. Players enter LACUNA, a metaverse space where the wildly popular online card game "Digimon Liberator" is played.

The fiction gives the cards a physical-feeling home. Duels are fought using a deck case–shaped gadget called a D-STORAGE, and players dive in through a household full-dive VR headset known as a T.A.L.E. It is a clever framing device: the tutorial, the deck building, and the ranked ladder all exist inside the story rather than beside it.

The official site introduces Kanata Hondo, a veteran gamer invited by her online friend Futre to try the new release, alongside the character Valner Dragnogh. Her arc — a longtime Digimon fan who never had many chances to actually play the card game — mirrors the experience of plenty of real-world players picking up a digital card game for the first time.
