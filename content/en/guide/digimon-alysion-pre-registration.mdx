## Frequently Asked Questions About Digimon Alysion Pre Registration

Yes. Registering on the App Store or Google Play costs nothing. The game itself is free-to-play with in-app purchases, but pre-registration does not require a payment or a deposit of any kind.

**Do the milestone rewards stack, or do I only get the top tier?**
They stack by tier. Reaching 200,000 registered players unlocks the WarGreymon reward without removing the pack tickets or the earlier cards. Every tier you clear is added to your launch-day mail.

The official notice states that rewards are delivered through in-game mail sent out when the app releases. There is no earlier distribution, and you should check your mailbox promptly after launch.

**Can the Digimon Alysion pre registration campaign be changed or cancelled?**
Yes. The official notice explicitly states that campaign details and schedules are subject to change or cancellation without prior notice, so treat the tier list as the current plan rather than a guarantee.
