## What the Digimon Alysion Beta Was (and Why It Mattered)

A closed beta is not a demo. It is a controlled, invite-only test where the developers watch how real players break things. Bandai ran exactly that kind of test for Digimon Alysion, opening tester applications on **July 28, 2025** and later publishing a survey report on **September 17, 2025**. Between those two dates, invited players got their first real look at the battle loop on mobile hardware.

Why does that matter if you were not invited? Because a beta leaves a paper trail. Every notice, survey summary, and site update tells you something about what the team is polishing — and what they are still worried about.

A few things are worth stating plainly, because they shape how you should read any beta footage you find online:

- **The build was not final.** The official site explicitly notes that products are still in development and that images may differ from the released game.
- **Access was limited and regional.** Closed tests rarely cover every territory at once, so "I couldn't get in" was the normal experience, not a bug.
- **The monetization model was already public.** Digimon Alysion is free-to-play with in-app purchases, which means a beta is also where pricing pacing and progression gates get stress-tested.

## Reading the Closed Beta Survey Report

Bandai published a "Closed β Test Survey Report" on September 17, 2025. The news feed lists it as an official notice, but it does not reproduce the full findings in the feed itself, so treat specific numbers circulating on forums and social media as **community reports** rather than confirmed data.

That said, survey reports for mobile card games generally cover a predictable set of topics, and you can use them as a checklist for what to watch in the next test:

- **Connection stability and matchmaking speed** — can players find a match in under a minute on a spotty connection?
- **Card balance and meta health** — is one deck archetype steamrolling everything else?
- **Onboarding clarity** — do new players understand the D-STORAGE interface without a wiki?
- **Monetization sentiment** — do free-to-play players feel progress is possible without spending?
- **Battery and heat on mid-range phones** — the quiet killer of mobile card games.

If you want to influence the next round, this is your roadmap. Testers who file specific, reproducible feedback about matchmaking or balance tend to shape patches far more than testers who simply report "it feels laggy."

## Practical Tips From the Digimon Alysion Beta Cycle

A few habits separate players who consistently get into tests from players who find out three days late.

**Turn on notifications you actually read.** The Digimon Alysion beta application window opened on July 28, 2025, and the survey report followed roughly seven weeks later. That is a short cycle. If your only source is a YouTube video published after the fact, you will always be behind.

**Understand what "closed" means.** Closed tests are usually region-locked and device-limited. If a test opens in Japan only, a VPN does not make you a legitimate tester, and account bans during a beta can carry into launch.

**Judge footage by its date.** Beta builds change weekly. A clip from the first day of the Digimon Alysion beta may show balance decisions that were reverted before the survey report was even written.

**Expect resets.** Nearly every mobile card game wipes beta progress before launch. Play the test to learn systems, not to grind a collection.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Digimon Alysion Beta

**Is the Digimon Alysion beta still running?**
No. Tester applications opened on July 28, 2025, and Bandai published its Closed β Test Survey Report on September 17, 2025, which indicates that test cycle has concluded. No new beta has been announced as of this writing.

**How do I get into the next Digimon Alysion beta test?**
Pre-register on the App Store or Google Play, follow the official site's news feed and Discord, and enable notifications. When a new test opens, applications are typically announced through those channels first and fill quickly.

**Does pre-registering guarantee beta access?**
No. Pre-registration registers interest in the launch and usually unlocks campaign rewards, but it is a separate system from beta tester selection. The two often run at the same time, which is where the confusion comes from.

**Will my beta progress carry over to launch?**
Assume it will not. Mobile card games almost universally reset beta accounts before release, both to protect the economy and to give every launch player the same starting line.

**Is Digimon Alysion free to play?**
Yes. The official site lists it as free-to-play with in-app purchases, on mobile platforms.
