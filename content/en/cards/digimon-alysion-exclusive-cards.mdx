## Exclusive Cards vs. Standard Set Cards — and How to Prepare

Not every card in your collection will be scarce, and treating them all the same is a mistake. Here is how the two groups typically compare.

| Factor | Exclusive / promotional cards | Standard set cards |

| Reprint odds | Low to none | Moderate to high |
| Deck impact | Often narrow but powerful | Broad and format-defining |
| Collector value | High, driven by scarcity | Moderate, driven by playability |
| Best strategy | Never dismantle or trade away | Trade duplicates freely |

One more piece of advice: resist the urge to chase every exclusive. In a free-to-play economy, spreading resources across every limited event is the fastest way to end up with a shallow collection. Pick the two or three exclusives that fit the deck you actually want to play, and let the rest go.
