## Practical Tips for Reading Early Card Reveals

- **Anchor on Digimon, not stats.** Names and evolutionary lines are the most stable information; numbers get rebalanced constantly.
- **Watch for a faction keyword.** The Di:WO guild is the only named group so far. If a trait or tag appears on multiple cards, that's your first archetype.

- **Separate "revealed" from "playable."** A card shown in a cinematic may never ship in that form.
- **Keep a "cut" list.** Cards you decided against are useful later when the meta shifts.
