## Build Your Own Digimon Alysion All Cards Tracker

Because no official database exists yet, the most valuable thing you can do this month is build a lightweight tracker. A spreadsheet with the right columns beats a hundred bookmarked forum posts.

**Step 1: Set up the sheet.** Create one tab for confirmed cards and one for rumored or unrevealed entries. Never mix them.

**Step 2: Lock the columns.** Use a structure like this:

| Column | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Card name | Exact spelling as shown in the reveal |
| Category | Digimon, Tamer, Option, promo |
| Color / attribute | Deck-building filter |
| Level | Evolution line mapping |
| Play cost / evolution cost | Curve analysis |
| Effect text | Copy verbatim from the source image |
| Source URL | Where you saw it, with a date |
| Status | Confirmed / community-reported / rumor |
| Notes | Translation quirks, errata flags |

**Step 3: Date every entry.** A card shown in a July 2025 beta build may read differently at launch. Dating entries lets you spot outdated text instantly.

**Step 4: Screenshot the source.** Images disappear when campaigns end. A local screenshot of the original reveal is your proof when a wiki edit war breaks out.

**Step 5: Review weekly.** Ten minutes on the official news feed each week will keep your sheet ahead of almost everyone else in your play group.

## Deck Planning Before the Full List Drops

You do not need **Digimon Alysion all cards** in front of you to start preparing. What you need is a framework for evaluating whatever gets revealed.

- **Track the cost curve first.** Sort your confirmed cards by play cost and look for gaps. Early digital card games are usually won by the player who can act on turn two or three, not the one with the biggest finisher.
- **Count your search and draw effects.** Consistency beats raw power in a 60-card-style deck, and consistency comes from cards that find other cards.
- **Note every evolution line you can complete.** A partial line is a dead card in hand. Flag lines where you have the rookie and champion but no ultimate.
- **Separate "good in a vacuum" from "good in this pool."** A removal option that costs one more than the format's average play is often unplayable, even if the effect reads well.
- **Watch the beta feedback themes.** The Closed Beta Test Survey Report is the closest thing to a design roadmap you will get — it tells you which systems players found confusing or overpowered, and those are the systems most likely to be adjusted.

If you want the ground truth rather than summaries, read the [official Digimon Alysion site](https://www.digimon-alysion.com/en/) directly and follow its news feed. Everything else is commentary.

## Common Mistakes When Chasing the Card List

**Treating datamined lists as final.** Beta builds are pruned and rebalanced. A card that existed in July 2025 may never ship.

**Ignoring distribution cards.** Pre-registration campaigns frequently bundle exclusive cards. If you register late, you may permanently miss a promo — check the campaign notice dates, not just the game page.

**Confusing Digimon Liberator the fiction with Digimon Alysion the product.** Liberator is the in-world game Kanata plays; Alysion is the actual title you install. Card names from the fiction do not automatically appear in the digital game.

**Overbuilding before launch.** Community reports suggest the beta pool was narrow. Building a 60-card masterpiece around eight confirmed cards guarantees disappointment — build skeletons instead.

**Skipping the source dates.** Two conflicting wiki entries usually mean one is simply older. Always check when the text was captured.

## FAQ

**Is there an official list of Digimon Alysion all cards yet?**
No. As of the October 28, 2026 site update, Bandai has published pre-registration and campaign notices but no browsable card database. Any complete list circulating online is community-compiled and should be verified against official reveals.

**Where do community card lists come from?**
Mostly from closed beta participants, official social media posts, and campaign artwork. Community reports are useful for early deck planning, but treat every entry without an official source as provisional.

Almost certainly not. Beta builds are used to test systems, and the September 17, 2025 survey report shows Bandai was actively collecting feedback on balance. Expect adjustments, removals, and additions.

**Do I need every card to compete in Digimon Alysion?**
No. Free-to-play card games are designed so that focused deck-building beats collection size. Prioritize the evolution lines and support cards that fit one strategy rather than chasing completion.

**How often should I check for new reveals?**
Weekly is enough for most players. The official news feed is the only channel that matters for confirmed information, and it updates in bursts around campaign milestones rather than daily.
